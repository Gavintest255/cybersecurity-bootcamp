# **Lab: What is a Computer?**

# **Overview**

Understanding the internal components of a computer is crucial to managing both physical and virtual systems effectively. This lab introduces the basic hardware components of a computer, helping you understand their functions and their impact on system performance. Knowledge of physical components and their interactions will enable you to allocate resources appropriately in virtual environments like VirtualBox or cloud systems.

As you progress through the lab, you'll document the components of your lab computer, research their functions, and analyze their importance to the system's operation. You'll also explore troubleshooting steps for any errors encountered and how these components relate to virtual computing environments.

# **Objectives**

1. Research the internal components of your lab computer and understand their roles in system performance.  
2. Document your findings in a well-structured format with descriptions, purposes, and photos of each component.  
3. Identify and resolve any hardware-related errors, noting solutions and resources used during troubleshooting.
